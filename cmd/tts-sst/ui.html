Voices (TTS)

The voice that reads replies aloud. Downloaded voices work offline, forever.

Speech recognition (STT)

The model that turns what you say into text. Larger models are more accurate but slower.

Transcription

Batch speaker-diarized transcription: apps send a recording to this machine's IP and port and get back a transcript with named speakers.

Enrolled speakers

Voice profiles used to put names on speakers. Stored as portable files in %APPDATA%\tts-sst\enrollments — copy the folder to back them up or move machines.

Enroll a new speaker

Give it 45+ seconds of one person speaking, recorded on the same setup your meetings are recorded on — that matters more than studio quality.
